How a Camp Works

1. Register

Fill in a short form with your details and a health questionnaire.

2. Screening

Medical staff check your weight, blood pressure, pulse and hemoglobin.

3. Donate

The donation itself takes about 10 to 15 minutes.

4. Rest

Relax and enjoy refreshments before you leave.
